# Orbit 1.4 customer update

Use this skill to draft a short customer-facing email or talking points.

1. Read [the customer FAQ](references/customer-faq.md) for approved wording.
2. Highlight saved dashboard views and weekly summary exports.
3. Avoid promising availability dates, pricing, or performance improvements.
4. Finish with an invitation to request a demo; do not imply a contract or
   purchase commitment.

Example prompt: "Draft a customer email introducing Orbit 1.4."
